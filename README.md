# 🇮🇳 Indian Listed NCD & Corporate Bond Screener

A Python & Streamlit analytics screener for secondary market Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and listed corporate bonds in India.

## Features
- **Dynamic Derived Calculations**: Remaining tenor, liquidity flags (High/Medium/Low), risk categorization, and investor-tailored post-tax yield calculations.
- **Yield Curve & Visual Analytics**: Interactive Plotly scatter plot (Yield vs. Tenor sized by traded volume) and credit classification breakdowns.
- **Filtering Suite**: Issuer search, Sector, Rating, Collateral (Secured/Unsecured), Seniority, and YTM sliders.
- **Color Coded Opportunities**: Automatic highlighting of bonds that beat your target post-tax return benchmark.
- **Data Export**: Export your filtered shortlist to CSV.
